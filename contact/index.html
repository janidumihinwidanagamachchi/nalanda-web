JOINING THE COLLEGE

Admissions to Nalanda

Admissions are governed by the applicable Ministry of Education circulars and available places. Confirm current dates and eligibility directly with the college; this page is a guide, not an admission notice.

Grade 1 admissions

  1. Read the current Ministry of Education circular.
  2. Prepare the documents required by that circular.
  3. Submit through the officially announced process.
  4. Follow published interview and selection notices.

Grade 6 scholarship intake

  1. Check scholarship results and official cut-off marks.
  2. Review placement and appeal instructions.
  3. Confirm the documents needed by the college.
  4. Complete enrolment when placement is confirmed.

Grade 12 · Advanced Level

  1. Confirm the college’s current vacancies and streams.
  2. Check O/L results and subject-specific entry criteria.
  3. Prepare school records and transfer documents.
  4. Apply within the officially published application window.
